## 摘要
在本工作中，我们建立了一个统一的理论框架，用于分析基于旋转的矩阵优化器的收敛性。这类优化器应用正交变换将动量映射到旋转空间中，在该空间中进行逐坐标或归一化更新，然后再将更新旋转回去。我们的框架涵盖了若干重要的矩阵优化器，包括 SOAP、Conda 和截断 SPlus，并将矩阵参数化的 Adam 作为特例纳入其中。作为主要结果，我们首次建立了具有尖锐维度依赖性的 SOAP 收敛速率，以及以核范数度量的 Adam 收敛速率。我们的框架还涵盖了一类更一般的基于旋转的优化器变体，它允许使用任意正交旋转矩阵，并允许这些矩阵在每次迭代时依赖于当前随机梯度。在技术上，我们的框架利用行-列迹控制论证，将逐元素界转化为对两个对角控制矩阵的界，从而改进了所得界的维度依赖性。

## Abstract
In this work, we develop a unified theoretical framework for analyzing the convergence of rotation-based matrix optimizers, which apply orthogonal transformations to map the momentum into a rotated space, perform coordinate-wise or normalized updates there, and then rotate the updates back. Our framework encompasses prominent matrix optimizers, including SOAP, Conda, and truncated SPlus, as well as matrix-parameterized Adam as a special case. As our main result, we establish, for the first time, the convergence rate of SOAP with sharp dimensional dependence, as well as the convergence rate of Adam measured by the nuclear norm. Our framework also covers a more general variant of rotation-based optimizers that allows arbitrary orthogonal rotation matrices, allowing these matrices to depend on the current stochastic gradient at each iteration. Technically, our framework leverages a row-column trace-control argument that converts elementwise bounds into bounds on two diagonal control matrices, thereby improving the dimension dependence of the resulting bound.
