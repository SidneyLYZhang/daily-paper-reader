## 摘要
我们研究马尔可夫过程的随机化停时，其由循序可测强度 $α_t(ω)$ 描述。我们证明每个容许强度都有一个马尔可夫代表 $λ(t,X_t)$，该代表可由观测测度和存活占用测度显式得到。该代表同时保持存活质量测度族以及停时与停止时状态的联合分布。只要关于参考强度 $r(t,x)$ 的相对熵有限，我们就证明一个精确分解，表明马尔可夫化不增加熵。因此，随机化停时仅通过其观测测度和熵惩罚进入的变分问题，可约化为对马尔可夫强度 $λ(t,x)$ 的优化。对于每个具有有限熵代表的观测测度，存在唯一的极小熵随机化停时，且它是马尔可夫的。我们讨论其与最优 Skorokhod 嵌入的联系，并证明布朗停时的一个有限约束实现结果。我们还用有界马尔可夫强度生成的观测测度来逼近任意观测测度。

## Abstract
We study randomized stopping times for a Markov process, described by a progressively measurable intensity $α_t(ω)$. We prove that every admissible intensity has a Markovian representative $λ(t,X_t)$, explicitly obtained from the observed measure and the surviving occupation measure. This representative preserves both the family of surviving mass measures and the joint distribution of the stopping time and the state at stopping. Whenever the relative entropy with respect to a reference intensity $r(t,x)$ is finite, we prove an exact decomposition showing that Markovization does not increase the entropy.   As a consequence, variational problems in which a randomized stopping time enters only through its observed measure and an entropy penalty can be reduced to optimization over Markovian intensities $λ(t,x)$. For every observed measure admitting a finite-entropy representative, there is a unique minimum-entropy randomized stopping time, and it is Markovian. We discuss the connection with optimal Skorokhod embedding and prove a finite-constraint realization result for Brownian stopping. We also approximate arbitrary observed measures by those generated by bounded Markovian intensities.
