## 摘要
我们给出了带几何停止的极限五专家预测问题的一个解，基于随机分析与偏微分方程。若 $δ$ 为停止概率，则初始分数并列时的极小极大期望遗憾为 $45π^2/(512\sqrt{2δ})+o(δ^{-1/2})$，当 $δ\downarrow0$ 时。选择最好和第三好专家的对抗性排名控制在每个状态都最大化极限哈密顿量。遵循 Bayraktar、Ekren 和 Zhang (2020) 的四专家构造，我们将值修正表示为退化的斜反射布朗运动的折现边界局部时期望。支配其边界迹的双曲系统被推导并显式求解。为验证非线性 Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman 方程，我们结合等号方向、双曲比较原理以及对各控制取平均的方法。这些论证将 48 个区域控制不等式约化为低维边界问题，其余符号则由显式单调性论证确定。我们证明了跨越区域界面以及排名顺序变化处的全局 $C^2$ 正则性。我们还表明，交替 COMB 控制恰好当两个最高分数相等且第三高与第四高分数相等时达到极限哈密顿量最大值。

## Abstract
We present a solution of the limiting five-expert prediction problem with geometric stopping, based on stochastic calculus and partial differential equations. If $δ$ is the stopping probability, the minimax expected regret from tied initial scores is $45π^2/(512\sqrt{2δ})+o(δ^{-1/2})$ as $δ\downarrow0$. The adversarial rank control selecting the best and third-best experts maximizes the limiting Hamiltonian at every state. Following the four-expert construction of Bayraktar, Ekren, and Zhang (2020), we represent the value correction as a discounted boundary-local-time expectation for a degenerate obliquely reflected Brownian motion. The hyperbolic systems governing its boundary traces are derived and solved explicitly. To verify the nonlinear Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman equation, we combine equality directions, hyperbolic comparison principles, and averaging across controls. These arguments reduce the 48 regional control inequalities to lower-dimensional boundary problems, with the remaining signs established by explicit monotonicity arguments. We prove global $C^2$ regularity across both regional interfaces and changes in rank ordering. We also show that the alternating COMB control attains the limiting Hamiltonian maximum exactly when the two highest scores coincide and the third- and fourth-highest scores coincide.
