## 摘要
我们利用随机哈密顿系统的 Wong-Zakai 逼近构造变分积分器，以研究其动力学。该方法可用于解释并验证 Wang 等人（L. Wang, J. Hong, R. Scherer, F. Bai. Dynamics and variational Integrators of stochastic Hamiltonian systems. International Journal of Numerical Analysis and Modeling, 6(4), 2009）关于随机变分积分器的先前工作。文中给出了数值算例。

## Abstract
We construct variational integrators to study the dynamics of stochastic Hamiltonian systems using their Wong-Zakai approximation. This approach can be used to interpret and justify previous work of Wang et al (L. Wang, J. Hong, R. Scherer, F. Bai. Dynamics and variational Integrators of stochastic Hamiltonian systems. International Journal of Numerical Analysis and Modeling, 6(4), 2009) on stochastic variational integrators. Numerical examples are provided.
