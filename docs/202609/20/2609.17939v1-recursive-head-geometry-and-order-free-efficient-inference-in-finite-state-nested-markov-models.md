## 摘要
要利用嵌套马尔可夫模型所编码的等式约束，需要其切空间几何。对于任意无环有向混合图（ADMGs）上的严格正有限状态模型，我们对递归头坐标卡求微分，并证明其得分映射的值域为整个切空间。内蕴集坐标块构成代数直和，不同区的块相互正交，并且由此得到的 Gram 投影既不需要 mb 屏蔽性，也不需要区顺序。精确反例表明，仅凭观测中心化和核归一化并不能证明切性。对于此处考虑的节点、完全源边和相容的路径特定干预目标，边界代换产生一个归一化的配置活跃律和一个顺序无关的典范梯度公式，并通过独立源重抽推广到有限混合。一个具有精确余项恒等式的相干一步估计量，以及一个模型有效的坐标卡流目标最大似然估计量，在所述局部条件下是有效的。在一个更窄的源隔离固定节点子类上，一个序贯估计量具有精确的转移因子化漂移，并且在 $m$ 个活跃区转移上最多有 $2^m$ 个冗余参数正确性机制；其全正确影响函数投影到典范梯度上，并且一个精确有理分布律表现出严格的方差差距。这些结果将任意有限状态 ADMGs 上的顺序无关有效性与更窄构造的多重稳健性区分开来。

## Abstract
Exploiting the equality restrictions that nested Markov models encode requires their tangent-space geometry. For strictly positive finite-state models on arbitrary acyclic directed mixed graphs (ADMGs), we differentiate the recursive-head chart and prove that the range of its score map is the full tangent space. Intrinsic-set coordinate blocks form an algebraic direct sum, blocks of distinct districts are orthogonal, and the resulting Gram projection needs neither mb-shieldedness nor a district order. Exact counterexamples show that observational centering and kernel normalization alone do not certify tangency. For the node, complete-source edge, and compatible path-specific intervention targets considered here, boundary substitution yields a normalized configured active law and an order-free canonical-gradient formula, extended to finite mixtures by independent source redraws. A coherent one-step estimator with an exact remainder identity and a model-valid chart-flow targeted maximum likelihood estimator are efficient under stated local conditions. On a narrower source-isolated fixed-node subclass, a sequential estimator has an exact transition-factorized drift and up to $2^m$ nuisance-correctness regimes over $m$ active-district transitions; its all-correct influence function projects onto the canonical gradient, and an exact rational law exhibits a strict variance gap. These results separate order-free efficiency on arbitrary finite-state ADMGs from multiple robustness of a narrower construction.
