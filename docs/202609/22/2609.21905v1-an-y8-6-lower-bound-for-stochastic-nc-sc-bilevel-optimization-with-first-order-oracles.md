## 摘要
我们研究具有非凸上层目标和强凸下层问题的光滑双层优化问题中寻找 $ε$-稳定点的预言机复杂度。我们考虑一个随机一阶预言机，它返回上层和下层目标的随机梯度，二者均为无偏且方差由 $σ^2$ 界定。我们证明，对于任意初始最优性间隙 $Δ>0$ 以及所有充分小的 $ε>0$，任何自适应随机一阶算法都需要 $Ω\!\!\left(Δκ_y^2 ε^{-2}\max\{1,σ^2κ_y^6ε^{-4}\}\right)$ 次预言机查询，才能找到其超目标函数的 $ε$-稳定点，其中 $κ_y$ 表示下层问题的条件数。特别地，在噪声主导的情形下，该下界为 $Ω\!\!\left(Δσ^2κ_y^8ε^{-6}\right)$。这确立了最佳已知一阶随机方法所达到的 $ε^{-6}$ 依赖性的最优性。

## Abstract
We study the oracle complexity of finding $ε$-stationary points of smooth bilevel optimization problems with a nonconvex upper-level objective and a strongly convex lower-level problem. We consider a stochastic first-order oracle that returns unbiased stochastic gradients of both the upper- and lower-level objectives, with variance bounded by $σ^2$. We prove that, for any initial optimality gap $Δ>0$ and all sufficiently small $ε>0$, every adaptive randomized first-order algorithm requires $Ω\!\left(Δκ_y^2 ε^{-2}\max\{1,σ^2κ_y^6ε^{-4}\}\right)$ oracle queries to find an $ε$-stationary point of its hyper-objective function, where $κ_y$ denotes the condition number of the lower-level problem. In particular, in the noise-dominated regime, the lower bound is $Ω\!\left(Δσ^2κ_y^8ε^{-6}\right)$. This establishes the optimality of the $ε^{-6}$ dependence achieved by the best-known first-order stochastic methods.
