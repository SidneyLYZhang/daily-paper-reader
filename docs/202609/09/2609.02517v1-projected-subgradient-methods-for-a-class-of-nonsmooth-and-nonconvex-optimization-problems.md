## 摘要
我们研究了一类目标函数为满足非光滑版本下降引理的非光滑函数，且约束集为非空闭集但不要求凸集的优化问题。目标函数属于上-C^2函数类，而约束可能促进稀疏或低秩结构。我们提出了带有两种不同全局化策略的投影次梯度方法：(a) 非单调线搜索，以及在额外假设下的 (b) 自适应条件方法，其中步长由依赖于过去迭代数据的公式给出。我们证明了这两种方法均收敛于满足比从次微分和规则预期更强的平稳性概念的解，这一点尤为重要，因为我们关注的优化问题本质上是非凸的。最后，我们将算法应用于MPEC风格问题以及矩阵优化问题MAXCUT和鲁棒PCA，展示了有前景的数值结果。

## Abstract
We investigate the optimization problem of minimizing a nonsmooth function that satisfies a nonsmooth version of the descent lemma over a nonempty and closed but not necessarily convex set. The objective function belongs to the class of upper-$\mathcal{C}^2$ functions, whereas the constraints may promote a sparse or low-rank structure. We propose a projected subgradient method with two different globalization strategies: (a) a nonmonotone linesearch and, under additional assumptions, (b) an auto-conditioned method, where the stepsize is given by a formula depending on data from past iterations. We show that both methods converge to solutions that satisfy a stronger stationarity concept than one would expect from the subdifferential sum-rule, which is particularly important since the optimization problems of interest are inherently nonconvex. Finally, we present promising numerical results when applying the algorithm to an MPEC-style problem as well as the matrix optimization problems MAXCUT and Robust PCA.
