## 摘要
我们分析了用于过阻尼 Langevin 动力学的随机 Runge--Kutta 积分器的均方误差，其势函数在一个有界区域外是凸的。一种分解将局部误差拆分为均值为零的项和一个更小的余项，而离散 Poisson 方程将其矩转化为对时间平均误差的界。我们对 Yang 和 Wang 提出的两种随机 Runge--Kutta 积分器进行了这一分析；二者均具有强阶 $\frac32$ 和弱阶 2，并且只评估势函数的梯度而不使用更高阶导数。我们证明，它们的分布以 Wasserstein-1 距离二阶逼近精确解的分布（至多相差一个对数项），且在步数上一致。对于具有直到三阶有界导数的检验函数，我们证明，在步长为 $h$ 的 $N$ 步上的均方误差为 $\mathcal O ( \frac{1}{Nh} + h^4 )$，这是离散化中的最优阶。实验在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中的非凸势上测量强阶和弱阶以及采样偏差，并在 CIFAR-10 的扩散模型上比较这些积分器。

## Abstract
We analyze the mean square error of stochastic Runge--Kutta integrators for overdamped Langevin dynamics whose potential is convex outside a bounded region. A decomposition splits the local error into a mean-zero term and a smaller remainder, and the discrete Poisson equation turns their moments into a bound on the error of a time average. We carry this out for two stochastic Runge--Kutta integrators proposed by Yang and Wang, both of strong order $\frac32$ and weak order 2, evaluating the gradient of the potential and no higher derivative. We show that their laws approach the law of the exact solution at second order in Wasserstein-1 distance, up to a logarithm, uniformly in the number of steps. For a test function with bounded derivatives up to third order, we prove that the mean square error over $N$ steps with step size $h$ is $\mathcal O ( \frac{1}{Nh} + h^4 )$, which is the optimal order in the discretization. Experiments measure the strong and weak orders and the sampling bias on a nonconvex potential in $\mathbb R^2$, and compare the integrators on a diffusion model of CIFAR-10.
