## 摘要
本文研究一般非线性随机系统的采样数据镇定问题，其中连续时间闭环系统满足局部指数型 Lyapunov 条件。仅假设系数局部 Lipschitz，既不要求线性增长条件，也不要求 Khasminskii 型条件。两个反例表明，几乎必然镇定通常是无法实现的。相反，给定初始状态，我们证明在足够快的采样下可实现任意高概率的指数稳定性。针对 \(p\ge 2\) 和 \(0<p<2\) 分别发展了方法，其中 \(p\) 是 Lyapunov 条件中的增长阶。数值例子说明了所得结果。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study sampled-data stabilization of general nonlinear stochastic systems under a local exponential-type Lyapunov condition for the continuous-time closed-loop system. The coefficients are assumed only locally Lipschitz, with neither linear-growth nor Khasminskii-type conditions. Two counterexamples show that almost-sure stabilization is generally unattainable. Instead, given an initial state, we establish exponential stability with arbitrarily high probability under sufficiently fast sampling. Separate methods are developed for \(p\ge 2\) and \(0<p<2\), where \(p\) is the growth order in the Lyapunov condition. Numerical examples illustrate the results.
