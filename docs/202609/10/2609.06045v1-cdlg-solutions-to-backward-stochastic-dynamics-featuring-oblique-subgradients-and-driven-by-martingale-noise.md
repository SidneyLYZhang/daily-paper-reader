## 摘要
本研究改进了对一般完备带流概率空间上后向随机变分动力学的定性分析，该分析遵循 Liang、Lyons 和 Qian (2011) 的思想。我们的主要目标是克服 Bensoussan、Li 和 Yam (2018) 研究中的一个重大局限：其中施加于多值次微分算子的有界性条件排除了标准的障碍型约束和凸集的示性函数。我们在作为驱动的真下半连续凸函数仅被仿射/二次函数从下方界定的自然假设下，证明了强 càdlàg 解的存在唯一性。此外，秉承 Gassous、Răşcanu 和 Rotenstein (2012, 2015) 的开创性结果，我们引入了由时变、一致正定对称矩阵支配的斜反射。

## Abstract
The present study improves the qualitative analysis of backward stochastic variational dynamics on a general complete filtered probability space, considered in the spirit of Liang, Lyons and Qian (2011). Our primary objective is to overcome a substantial limitation in the study of Bensoussan, Li and Yam (2018), where the boundedness condition imposed on the multivalued subdifferential operator excludes standard obstacle-type constraints and indicator functions of convex sets. We prove the existence and uniqueness of a strong càdlàg solution under the natural assumption that the driving proper lower semicontinuous convex function is merely bounded from below by an affine/quadratic function. Furthermore, we incorporate an oblique reflection governed by a time-dependent, uniformly positive definite symmetric matrix, in the spirit of the pioneering results of Gassous, Răşcanu and Rotenstein (2012, 2015).
