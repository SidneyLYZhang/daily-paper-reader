## 摘要
我们证明，一阶算法在最坏情况下需要 $Ω(δ^{-1}ε^{-3})$ 次梯度查询，才能找到 Lipschitz 函数的 $(δ,ε)$-Goldstein 稳定点；在该点处，距离不超过 $δ$ 的梯度的某个凸组合的范数至多为 $ε$。该下界是紧的，与已知算法在绝对常数意义下相匹配，从而解决了非光滑非凸优化中收敛到稳定点的复杂度问题。我们进一步证明，对于寻找满足最近提出的松弛 $(λ,ε)$-稳定性概念的点，存在 $Ω(λ^{1/2}ε^{-7/2})$ 的紧下界；该概念允许组合距离更远的梯度。我们的结果表明，收敛到非光滑稳定点的速率不受梯度随机性的影响，这与光滑优化形成鲜明对比。

## Abstract
We prove that first-order algorithms require $Ω(δ^{-1}ε^{-3})$ gradient queries (in the worst case) to find a $(δ,ε)$-Goldstein stationary point of a Lipschitz function, at which there is a convex combination of gradients within distance $δ$ whose norm is at most $ε$. This lower bound is tight, matching known algorithms up to absolute constants, therefore resolving the complexity of convergence to stationarity in nonsmooth nonconvex optimization. We further prove a tight lower bound of $Ω(λ^{1/2}ε^{-7/2})$ for finding points satisfying the recently proposed relaxed notion of $(λ,ε)$-stationarity, which allows combining further-away gradients. Our results reveal that convergence rates to nonsmooth stationarity are not affected by gradient stochasticity, in sharp contrast to smooth optimization.
