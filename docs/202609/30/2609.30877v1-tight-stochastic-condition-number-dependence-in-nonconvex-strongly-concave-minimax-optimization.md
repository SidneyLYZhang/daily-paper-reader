## 摘要
我们研究 SAPD+ 的随机复杂度中对条件数的线性依赖对于非凸-强凹极小极大优化是否必要。对于联合 L-光滑且对偶强凹参数为 μ 的目标函数，我们证明了一个下界，在相同的 Moreau 包络驻点性准则和相同的原始-对偶初始化间隙下，该下界与 SAPD+ 的上界相匹配。具体而言，当 σ≥ε 时，在所述精度范围内，zero-respecting 算法的最坏情况复杂度为 Θ(κLGσ^2ε^{-4})，其中 κ=L/μ，G 界定初始原始-对偶间隙，σ^2 界定一般无偏一阶 oracle 的方差。该下界在一个具有有界对偶盒的光滑问题类上实现。我们的构造将非凸零链的每一环都通过一个幅度与 ε/√κ 成正比的对偶梯度来传递，而一个未被发现的原始坐标阻止了驻点性。将其与已知确定性界结合后，它还给出原始梯度下界 Ω(LΔ(√κε^{-2}+κσ^2ε^{-4}))，其中 Δ 界定初始原始函数间隙。

## Abstract
We study whether the linear condition-number dependence in the stochastic complexity of SAPD+ is necessary for nonconvex-strongly-concave minimax optimization. For jointly $L$-smooth objectives with dual strong-concavity parameter $μ$, we prove a lower bound that matches the SAPD+ upper bound under the same Moreau-envelope stationarity criterion and the same primal-dual initialization gap. Specifically, when $σ\ge\varepsilon$, the worst-case complexity of zero-respecting algorithms is $Θ(κLGσ^2\varepsilon^{-4})$ in the stated accuracy regime, where $κ=L/μ$, $G$ bounds the initial primal-dual gap, and $σ^2$ bounds the variance of a general unbiased first-order oracle. The lower bound is realized on a smooth problem class with a bounded dual box. Our construction routes each link of a nonconvex zero-chain through a dual gradient of magnitude proportional to $\varepsilon/\sqrtκ$, while an undiscovered primal coordinate prevents stationarity. It also yields the primal-gradient lower bound $Ω(LΔ(\sqrtκ\varepsilon^{-2}+κσ^2\varepsilon^{-4}))$ after combination with the known deterministic bound, where $Δ$ bounds the initial primal function gap.
