## 摘要
一个正则观测统计模型可能收敛到一个极限，其中先前可识别的带符号参数仅在模反射意义下可识别。我们研究这一转变的局部信息几何。对于具有精确极限反射的二次可微 Hellinger 嵌入，观测位移被迫取二阶射流形式 ελJ_- + λ^2 J_+/2，直至高阶项。混合射流在远离对称面处恢复符号，而偶射流是商所继承的第一切向量。在消去冗余参数后，正 Gram 行列式给出非退化交叉帽二阶射流。相关的局部渐近理论有三个由 τ_n = √n ε_n^2 支配的机制：正则带符号 LAN、临界弯曲高斯子实验，以及具有 n^{-1/4} 符号尺度的商机制。我们证明，同一抛物型临界实验在沿单一平稳相依轨道的预测似然中持续存在。极限商在不变坐标中具有正则 Fisher 度量，而其拉回在符号坐标中退化。对于由相干海杂波观测激发的可解 CIR--OU 基准，我们显式推导出商 Fisher 度量和曲率，并证明曲率严格为负。我们还确定了完整 Amari 族的限制和乐：当 a = 0 时，Hol_0(∇^(a)) = SO(2)；而当 a ≠ 0 时，Hol_0(∇^(a)) = GL^+(2,ℝ)。这些结果将极限商的内蕴几何与其弱开折的横截几何区分开来。

## Abstract
A regular observed statistical model may converge to a limit in which a previously identifiable signed parameter becomes identifiable only modulo a reflection. We study the local information geometry of this transition. For a twice differentiable Hellinger embedding with an exact limiting reflection, the observed displacement is forced into the two-jet form \(\varepsilonλJ_-+λ^2J_+/2\), up to higher-order terms. The mixed jet restores the sign away from the symmetric face, whereas the even jet is the first tangent inherited by the quotient. After nuisance elimination, a positive Gram determinant yields a nondegenerate cross-cap two-jet. The associated local asymptotic theory has three regimes governed by \(τ_n=\sqrt n\,\varepsilon_n^2\): regular signed LAN, a critical curved Gaussian subexperiment, and a quotient regime with the \(n^{-1/4}\) signed scale. We prove that the same parabolic critical experiment persists for predictive likelihoods along a single stationary dependent trajectory. The limiting quotient has a regular Fisher metric in the invariant coordinate, while its pullback degenerates in the signed coordinate. For a solvable CIR--OU benchmark motivated by coherent sea-clutter observations, we derive the quotient Fisher metric and curvature explicitly and show that the curvature is strictly negative. We also determine the restricted holonomy of the full Amari family: \(\operatorname{Hol}_0(\nabla^{(a)})=SO(2)\) for \(a=0\), whereas \(\operatorname{Hol}_0(\nabla^{(a)})=GL^+(2,\mathbb R)\) for \(a\neq0\). The results separate the intrinsic geometry of the limiting quotient from the transverse geometry of its weak unfolding.

## 6. 主要结论与发现

- **Hellinger 二射流正常形式**：反射对称强制观测位移分裂为奇混合射流 ελJ₋ 与偶商射流 λ²J₊/2。
- **非退化 cross-cap 二射流**：消去冗余后，Δ_eff>0 保证有效二阶射流为 (ε, ελ, λ²/2)。
- **三机制渐近**：τ_n=√n ε_n² 控制正则带符号 LAN、临界弯曲高斯抛物实验、商机制（n^{−1/4} 符号尺度是 μ 坐标 n^{−1/2} 尺度的拉回）。
- **记忆下的持久性**：预测似然分解中两射流为鞅差分，临界几何在单条平稳轨迹上幸存，记忆仅变形 Gram 度量。
- **商与开折的几何分离**：限制到对称面会抹去混合射流 J_mix；而 cross-cap 二射流不能确定商的内蕴曲率（需更高偶射流）。
- **CIR–OU 基准显式结果**：
  - 商 Fisher 度量在边界非退化；
  - 边界高斯曲率严格为负：
