## 摘要
我们研究一个离散时间 Moran 过程的大种群极限，该过程具有多个策略，策略取自可能无限的策略空间 $\mathcal{V}$（一个度量空间），并同时考虑弱选择和强选择。在欧拉密度表述中，极限动力学关键取决于种群规模、突变率和选择强度之间的相对标度。根据这些标度关系，极限行为或由纯确定性的复制子型连续性方程支配，或由扩散增强的偏微分方程支配。在后一种情形中，扩散算子将经典 Fleming--Viot 算子和 Kimura 方程作为特例恢复出来。在方法上，我们通过在欧拉坐标中重新解释离散过程、构造满足近似偏微分方程的插值曲线，并在 $\mathcal{V}$ 上的概率分布之上的概率测度空间中的合适拓扑下通过紧性论证建立收敛性。

## Abstract
We study the large-population limit of a discrete-time Moran process featuring multiple strategies, drawn from a possibly infinite strategy space $\mathcal{V}$ (a metric space), under both weak and strong selection. In the Eulerian density formulation, the limiting dynamics depend critically on the relative scaling of population size, mutation rate, and selection intensity. Depending on these scalings, the limit behavior is governed either by a purely deterministic replicator-type continuity equation or by a diffusion-enhanced PDE. In the latter case, the diffusion operator recovers the classical Fleming--Viot operator and the Kimura equation as special instances. Methodologically, we derive the limit by reinterpreting the discrete process in Eulerian coordinates, constructing interpolating curves that satisfy an approximate PDE, and establishing convergence via a compactness argument in a suitable topology on the space of probability measures over probabilities over $\mathcal{V}$.
