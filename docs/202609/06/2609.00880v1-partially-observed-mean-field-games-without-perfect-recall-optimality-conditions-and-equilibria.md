## 摘要
本文研究不完美记忆（WPR）下的部分观测平均场博弈。代表性智能体观测到一个含噪信号，但时刻\(t\)的控制仅使用\(\mathcal G_t^I=σ(y_t)\)，这是一个通常非嵌套的信息族。条件总体律则使用观测滤波\(\mathbb F^Y\)。这些耦合层面依赖于不同的信息尺度，难以在单一构造中闭合。我们通过状态、驱动变量和随机平均场项的确定性兼容联合律对环境进行参数化，从而在不扩大智能体控制信息的情况下保持其依赖结构。对于固定律，参考测度和Girsanov定理导出一个WPR随机最大值原理；所选择的响应由条件哈密顿量和WPR信念测度表示。隐藏状态和平均场项的联合路径后验给出了条件总体律的弱Kushner-Stratonovich表示。递归响应映射在紧凸的兼容律集合上连续，因此Schauder-Tychonoff定理产生一个弱WPR均衡。对于固定的均衡律和反馈，兼容的Yamada-Watanabe定理将路径唯一性提升为强实现。最后，一个线性二次银行间借贷实例比较了完美记忆（PR）与WPR。PR响应遵循Kalman-Bucy反馈，而WPR响应求解Fredholm-Volterra方程，并在高斯性下关于当前观测是仿射的。数值实验说明了PR与WPR在均衡行为上的差异。

## Abstract
This paper studies partially observed mean field games without perfect recall (WPR). The representative agent observes a noisy signal, but the control at time \(t\) uses only \(\mathcal G_t^I=σ(y_t)\), a generally non-nested information family. The conditional population law instead uses the observation filtration \(\mathbb F^Y\). These coupled levels rely on different information scales and are difficult to close within one construction. We parameterize the environment by a deterministic compatible joint law of state, driving variables, and random mean field term, thereby preserving its dependence structure without enlarging the agent's control information. For a fixed law, a reference measure and Girsanov's theorem yield a WPR stochastic maximum principle; the selected response is represented by the conditional Hamiltonian and WPR belief measure. The joint path posterior of hidden state and mean field term gives a weak Kushner-Stratonovich representation of the conditional population law. A recursive response map is continuous on a compact convex set of compatible laws, so Schauder-Tychonoff yields a weak WPR equilibrium. For a fixed equilibrium law and feedback, a compatible Yamada-Watanabe theorem lifts pathwise uniqueness to a strong realization. Finally, a linear-quadratic interbank lending example compares perfect recall (PR) with WPR. The PR response follows the Kalman-Bucy feedback, whereas the WPR response solves a Fredholm-Volterra equation and is affine in the current observation under Gaussianity. The numerical experiment illustrates how equilibrium behavior differs between PR and WPR.
