## 摘要
我们研究具有多维强混合协变量过程的随机微分方程中扩散矩阵的非参数估计。我们提出一个基于一般函数类的灵活统计框架，该框架不需要线性基表示，从而使我们的结果可直接应用于深度神经网络估计器。我们的方法采用两步估计程序：构造初步的非参数拟似然估计量，随后通过 β-Hölder 类近似对其进行正则化。我们建立了任意估计量的经验风险与泛化风险之间的一般风险比较定理，而不依赖于底层过程的特定概率结构。在基于时间区间 [0,T] 上 n+1 个观测的扩散矩阵学习中，推导出的上界刻画了与协变量过程混合行为相关的 T 速率和支配波动率估计的内在 n 速率之间的内在相互作用。

## Abstract
We investigate the nonparametric estimation of the diffusion matrix in stochastic differential equations featuring multidimensional, strong mixing covariate processes. We propose a flexible statistical framework based on general function classes that does not require a linear basis representation, rendering our results directly applicable to deep neural network estimators. Our approach employs a two-step estimation procedure: constructing a preliminary nonparametric quasi-likelihood estimator and subsequently regularizing it via a $β$-Hölder class approximation. We establish general risk comparison theorems between empirical and generalization risks for arbitrary estimators without relying on a specific probabilistic structure of the underlying process. In diffusion matrix learning based on \(n + 1\) observations over the time interval \([0,T]\), the derived upper bounds capture the intrinsic interplay between the \(T\)-rate, associated with the mixing behavior of the covariate process, and the intrinsic \(n\)-rate governing the volatility estimation.
