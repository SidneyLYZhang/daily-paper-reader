## 摘要
我们推导了离散时间无限时域折扣随机最优控制问题的样本近似统计极限定理。第一个结果是，在总体最优策略的唯一性条件下，基于样本的值函数的泛函中心极限定理。极限分布是一个均值为零的高斯过程，其特征是一个类似于动态规划原理的线性不动点方程。我们将这些渐近性与基于样本的策略优化所得到的渐近性进行比较，并说明它们的极限方差可能不同。我们还推导了非唯一最优策略模型的极限定理，其中极限分布可能是非高斯的。在库存控制和可再生资源收割中的应用说明了该理论。

## Abstract
We derive statistical limit theorems for sample-based approximations of infinite-horizon discounted stochastic optimal control problems in discrete time. Our first result is a functional central limit theorem for the sample-based value function under a uniqueness-type condition on population optimal policies. The limiting law is a mean-zero Gaussian process characterized by a linear fixed point equation that resembles a dynamic programming principle. We compare these asymptotics with those obtained from sample-based policy optimization and illustrate that their limiting variances can be different. We also derive a limit theorem for models with nonunique optimal policies, where the limiting law may be non-Gaussian. Applications to inventory control and renewable harvesting illustrate the theory.
