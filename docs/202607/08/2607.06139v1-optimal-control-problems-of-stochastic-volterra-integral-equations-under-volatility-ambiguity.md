## 摘要
本文研究波动模糊性下由G-布朗运动驱动的随机Volterra积分方程的最优控制问题。借助G-随机分析技术和弱收敛方法，我们得到了代价泛函的变分和变分不等式。在凸性假设下，我们建立了随机最大值原理，该原理既是最优控制的必要条件也是充分条件。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study the optimal control problems for stochastic Volterra integral equations driven by G-Brownian motion under Volatility Ambiguity. With the help of G-stochastic analysis techniques and the weak convergence methods, we obtain the variation of the cost functional and the variational inequality. Under the convexity assumptions, we establish the stochastic maximum principle, which serves as both a necessary and sufficient condition for optimal control.
