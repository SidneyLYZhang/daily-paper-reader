## 摘要
我们研究了无正则化策略镜像下降（PMD）在有限折扣马尔可夫决策过程中采用任意常数步长时的策略收敛性。我们关注形式为 $h(p)=\sum_a ψ(p(a))$ 的可分解镜像映射，其中 $ψ$ 满足标准的勒让德型假设。在这些条件下，我们证明了 PMD 生成的策略序列在策略域中收敛到最优策略的极限，即使最优策略集不是单点集。这一结果涵盖了广泛使用的镜像映射类别，包括投影 Q 上升背后的平方欧几里得镜像映射、softmax 自然策略梯度背后的负香农熵、Tsallis 熵、Hellinger 映射以及费米-狄拉克熵。尽管策略收敛性此前已在特定 PMD 实例或正则化变体（如同伦 PMD）中得到证明，但据我们所知，这是首个针对一般可分解镜像映射和任意常数步长的无正则化 PMD 的系统性统一策略收敛理论。我们的分析进一步揭示，收敛行为由 $ψ$ 在 $0$ 和 $1$ 处的可微性决定，从而产生不同行为，包括有限时间终止、渐近收敛以及依赖于 MDP 的二分法。当 $ψ$ 二次连续可微且具有严格正有限曲率时，我们进一步为渐近收敛情况建立了局部策略收敛速率，涵盖了上述标准镜像映射。

## Abstract
We study the policy convergence of unregularized policy mirror descent (PMD) with arbitrary constant step sizes for finite discounted Markov decision processes. We focus on decomposable mirror maps of the form $h(p)=\sum_a ψ(p(a))$, where $ψ$ satisfies standard Legendre-type assumptions. Under these conditions, we prove that the policy sequence generated by PMD converges in the policy domain to a limiting optimal policy, even when the optimal policy set is not a singleton. This result covers a broad class of commonly used mirror maps, including the squared Euclidean mirror map underlying projected Q-ascent, the negative Shannon entropy underlying softmax natural policy gradient, Tsallis entropy, the Hellinger mapping, and the Fermi-Dirac entropy. Although policy convergence has been established previously for specific PMD instances or for regularized variants such as homotopic PMD, to the best of our knowledge, this is the first systematic and unified policy convergence theory for unregularized PMD under general decomposable mirror maps and arbitrary constant step sizes. Our analysis further reveals that the convergence behavior is governed by the differentiability of $ψ$ at $0$ and $1$, leading to different behaviors, including finite-time termination, asymptotic convergence, and an MDP-dependent dichotomy. When $ψ$ is twice continuously differentiable with strictly positive finite curvature, we further establish local policy convergence rates for the asymptotic convergence cases, covering the standard mirror maps mentioned above.
