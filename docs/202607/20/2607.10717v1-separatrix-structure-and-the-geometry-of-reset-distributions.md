## 摘要
我们研究了具有几何重置的吸收马尔可夫过程的重置分布的几何，在抽象层面上分离出重置中性不变性的结构机制。我们证明了谱对偶性赋予重置分布的单形以非平凡的谱响应几何：单形Δ_{m-1}承载着由耦合泛函C的水平集构成的叶状结构，围绕临界流形Σ组织，该流形作为重置响应的全局定向边界。在耦合泛函的四个结构条件(S1)-(S4)下，我们建立了分界线Σ的存在性和显式刻画，推导出不变值C^* = 1/(1+√K)，识别了谱系数中的射影结构，并在两位置情形证明了全局符号原理。线性泛函ψ(γ)作为全局定向场出现：数值证据表明对于所有π∈Δ_{m-1}^∘，有sgn(∂_γC) = sgn⟨π-π^*, ψ(γ)⟩。带多位点几何重置的有偏随机游走提供了一个典型实现。这是将随机重置与谱论和信息几何联系起来的系列论文的第三篇。

## Abstract
We study the geometry of reset distributions for absorbed Markov processes with geometric resetting, working at an abstract level that isolates the structural mechanism underlying reset-neutral invariance. We show that the spectral duality endows the simplex of reset distributions with a non-trivial spectral response geometry: the simplex $Δ_{m-1}$ carries a foliation by level sets of the coupling functional $C$, organized around a critical manifold $Σ$ that acts as a global orientation boundary for the reset response. Under four structural conditions (S1)--(S4) on the coupling functional, we establish the existence and explicit characterization of the separatrix $Σ$, derive the invariant value $C^* = 1/(1+\sqrt{K})$, identify a projective structure in the spectral coefficients, and prove a global sign principle in the two-site case. The linear functional $ψ(γ)$ emerges as a global orientation field: numerical evidence suggests $\operatorname{sgn}(\partial_γC) = \operatorname{sgn}\langleπ-π^*,ψ(γ)\rangle$ for all $π\in Δ_{m-1}^\circ$. The biased random walk with multi-site geometric resetting provides a canonical realization. This is the third paper in a program connecting stochastic resetting with spectral theory and information geometry.
