## 摘要
我们研究了带梯度非线性的随机抛物型方程的适定性。我们的分析基于临界空间中非线性随机抛物型方程的最新最大正则性框架。通过分别控制漂移和噪声系数，我们扩展了现有结果。这样，在次临界色散系数的情况下，我们可以允许较低正则性的驱动噪声。基于局部解的粘合方法，我们的方法还蕴含了新的延拓准则。然后，我们应用存在性结果和延拓准则来证明移动边界问题的相场模型的全局适定性。

## Abstract
We study well-posedness of stochastic parabolic equations with gradient nonlinearities. Our analysis is based on recent maximal-regularity frameworks for nonlinear stochastic parabolic equations in critical spaces. We extend the existing results by controlling drift and noise coefficient separately. This way we can allow for less regular driving noise in case of subcritical dispersion coefficients. Our approach, based on gluings of local solutions, moreover implies new continuation criteria. We then apply our existence result and the continuation criteria to show global well-posedness of phase-field models of moving boundary problems.
