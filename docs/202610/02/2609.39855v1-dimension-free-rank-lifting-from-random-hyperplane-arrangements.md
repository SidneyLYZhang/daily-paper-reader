## 摘要
我们研究神经网络随机初始化隐藏层实现秩提升所需的宽度。具体而言，给定数据集 $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$，其中包含 $m$ 个 $d$ 维输入向量，且这些向量两两夹角至少为 $θ$，我们考虑随机特征矩阵 $σ(XR)$，其中 $R$ 为标准高斯矩阵。对于正齐次非多项式激活函数（包括 sign、Heaviside、ReLU 以及 ReLU 的幂等），我们证明 $$n \gtrsim \frac{1}θ\max\left\{m,\log\left(\frac{1}δ\right)\right\}$$ 个神经元足以使 $σ(XR)$ 以至少 $1-δ$ 的概率具有满行秩 $m$。这一无维数界指数级改进了此前针对 sign 特征的一般维数保证（Drago et al., 2026），并且本质上是紧的。证明表明，单个随机特征列以概率 $Ω(θ)$ 逃逸出 $\mathbb{R}^m$ 的每一个真子空间；其证明使用了邻近高斯方向的耦合以及诱导超平面排列的局部穿越。我们还研究稳定秩提升，其目标是建立精确秩提升的定量类似物，即以高概率给出经验特征 Gram 矩阵最小特征值的下界。我们的分析统一并推广了所有 $q$-齐次非多项式激活函数的稳定秩保证，延续了 Panigrahi et al. (2020) 和 Song (2026) 的先前工作。特别地，我们将总体核的对角占优泰勒尾项与截断和矩阵集中相结合，证明对于正齐次非多项式激活函数，稳定秩提升在宽度 $$n \gtrsim C^q \frac{m}{θ^{2q+1}} \log^{2q+\frac{1}{2}}\left(\frac{m}θ\right) \log\left(\frac{m}δ\right),$$ 时实现，其中 $q$ 是激活函数的次数，$C > 0$ 是某个通用常数。

## Abstract
We study the width required for a randomly initialized hidden layer of a neural network to achieve rank lifting. Namely, given a dataset $X \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$ of $m$, $d$-dimensional input vectors separated by an angle of at least $θ$, we consider the random feature matrix $σ(XR)$, where $R$ is standard Gaussian. For positively homogeneous nonpolynomial activations, which include sign, Heaviside, ReLU, and ReLU powers among others, we prove that $$n \gtrsim \frac{1}θ\max\left\{m,\log\left(\frac{1}δ\right)\right\}$$ neurons suffice for $σ(XR)$ to have full row rank $m$ with probability at least $1-δ$. This dimension-free bound exponentially improves the previous general-dimensional guarantee for sign features (Drago et al., 2026) and is essentially tight. The proof shows that one random feature column escapes every proper subspace of $\mathbb{R}^m$ with probability $Ω(θ)$, using a coupling of nearby Gaussian directions and a local crossing of the induced hyperplane arrangement. We also study stable rank lifting, where the goal is to establish a quantitative analogue of exact rank lifting, i.e., a lower bound on the smallest eigenvalue of the empirical feature Gram matrix in high-probability.   Our analysis unifies and generalizes stable rank guarantees for all $q$-homogeneous non-polynomial activations following prior work in Panigrahi et al. (2020) and Song (2026). In particular, we combine a diagonally dominant Taylor tail of the population kernel with truncation and matrix concentration, to show that for positively homogeneous nonpolynomial activations, stable rank lifting is achieved at width $$n \gtrsim C^q \frac{m}{θ^{2q+1}} \log^{2q+\frac{1}{2}}\left(\frac{m}θ\right) \log\left(\frac{m}δ\right),$$ where $q$ is the degree of the activation and $C > 0$ is some universal constant.
