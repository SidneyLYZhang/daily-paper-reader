## 摘要
尽管最大编码率降低（MCR$^2$）目标已成为学习紧致且具有判别力的表示的一种有用准则，但对其优化几何的理论理解仍然有限，尤其是关于为何一阶方法在其局部极大值附近往往表现出快速收敛。在这项工作中，我们表明 MCR$^2$ 目标在其局部极大值的某个邻域内满足误差界性质。该性质保证到局部极大值集合的距离可以由该邻域内任意一点的梯度范数从上方界定，直接蕴含诸如 Polyak--Łojasiewicz 不等式和二次增长等正则性条件。利用该性质，我们证明在温和条件下，一阶方法线性收敛到 MCR$^2$ 目标的某个局部极大值。数值实验验证了所预测的局部收敛行为，并提供了经验证据，表明优化 MCR$^2$ 目标在保持有竞争力的下游精度的同时改善了表示几何。

## Abstract
While the maximal coding rate reduction (MCR$^2$) objective has become a useful principle for learning compact and discriminative representations, a theoretical understanding of its optimization geometry remains limited, especially regarding why first-order methods often exhibit fast convergence near its local maximizers. In this work, we show that the MCR$^2$ objective satisfies an error-bound property in a neighborhood of its local maximizers. This property guarantees that the distance to the local maximizer set can be bounded above by the gradient norm at any point in the neighborhood, directly implying regularity conditions such as the Polyak--Łojasiewicz inequality and quadratic growth. Leveraging this property, we prove that first-order methods converge linearly to a local maximizer of the MCR$^2$ objective under mild conditions. Numerical experiments validate the predicted local convergence behavior and provide empirical evidence that optimizing the MCR$^2$ objective improves representation geometry while maintaining competitive downstream accuracy.
