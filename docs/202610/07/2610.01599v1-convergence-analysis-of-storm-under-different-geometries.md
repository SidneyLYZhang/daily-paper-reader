## 摘要
随机递归动量（STORM）通过方差缩减效应在非凸优化中实现了快速收敛，但现有分析依赖于较强的平均光滑性假设。本文研究STORM在不使用平均光滑性时针对不同目标的收敛性。我们首先重新审视平均光滑性下的结果，得到非凸目标的 $O(T^{-1/3})$ 界，以及在 $\mu$-Polyak--Łojasiewicz（PL）条件下最后迭代输出的 $O(\sigma^2/(\mu T))$ 界。在不使用平均光滑性时，我们设计了一个辅助序列，并在分析中将其与STORM更新进行比较。借助该序列，我们证明STORM对非凸目标仍可达到 $O(T^{-1/4})$ 的速率，该速率在标准光滑性下是最优的。对于凸目标和 $\lambda$-强凸目标，我们进一步证明了平均迭代和最后迭代界，其最优速率分别为 $O(\sigma R/\sqrt T)$ 和 $O(\sigma^2/(\lambda T))$。所有获得的结果均使用相同的STORM递推，但采用不同的超参数选择。

## Abstract
Stochastic recursive momentum (STORM) achieves fast convergence for nonconvex optimization via the variance reduction effect, but existing analyses rely on the strong average smoothness assumption. In this paper, we study the convergence of STORM for different objectives without average smoothness. We first revisit the results under average smoothness, obtaining the $O(T^{-1/3})$ bound for nonconvex objectives and the $O(σ^2/(μT))$ bound for last-iterate output under the $μ$-Polyak--Łojasiewicz~(PL) condition. Without average smoothness, we design an auxiliary sequence and compare the STORM update with it in the analysis. With the help of this sequence, we prove that STORM still attains an $O(T^{-1/4})$ rate for nonconvex objectives, which is optimal under standard smoothness. For convex and $λ$-strongly convex objectives, we further prove averaged and last-iterate bounds with optimal rates of $O(σR/\sqrt T)$ and $O(σ^2/(λT))$, respectively. All the obtained results use the same STORM recursion with different hyperparameter choices.
